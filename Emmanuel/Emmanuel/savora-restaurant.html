Our Chef

Meet Chef Marcus Laurent

With over two decades of culinary experience across Michelin-starred kitchens in Paris, New York, and Tokyo, Chef Marcus brings a global perspective to every dish at Savora.

His philosophy is simple: respect the ingredients, honor the seasons, and create moments of joy through food. Every menu reflects his commitment to sustainability, innovation, and the timeless art of hospitality.

— Chef Marcus
