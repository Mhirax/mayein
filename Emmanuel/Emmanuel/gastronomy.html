Meet the Chef

Cooking with passion and purpose.

With over 15 years of culinary experience across Michelin-starred kitchens in Paris, Rome, and New York, Chef Marco brings a global perspective to every recipe.

His philosophy is simple: use the freshest ingredients, respect traditional techniques, and always cook with love. Every recipe on Gastronomy has been tested, refined, and perfected in his home kitchen.

When he's not cooking, you'll find him exploring local farmers' markets, experimenting with new flavors, or teaching cooking classes to aspiring home chefs.

— Chef Marco
