About Me

The eye behind the lens.

I'm Emma Cole, a fine art and editorial photographer based in Portland, Oregon. For over a decade, I've been capturing the raw beauty of human connection, natural landscapes, and the quiet moments that often go unnoticed.

My work has been featured in Vogue, National Geographic, and Harper's Bazaar. I believe that every photograph should evoke emotion, tell a story, and preserve a moment in time that can never be recreated.

When I'm not behind the camera, you'll find me hiking the Pacific Northwest trails, exploring antique shops, or sipping coffee at my favorite local café.

— Emma Cole
