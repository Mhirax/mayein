Positive youth development, since 2012

MAYEIN is a Nigerian non-profit advancing positive youth development through education, entrepreneurship, gender equity and civic engagement. Since 2012 we’ve reached over 23,000 young people, and hold special consultative status at the UN Economic and Social Council.

Read our story

Our Mission

To lead strategies that promote inclusive quality education and lifelong learning for all, gender equity, and civic readiness for the attainment of positive youth development.

Our Vision

A country and continent where young people are thriving in communities, and communities are thriving because of young people.

What we work on

  • Education

    Community-based access to literacy resources and in-school projects for underserved children and early youths.

    Explore →

  • Entrepreneurship

    Equipping young people with the skills and resources to launch and grow successful businesses.

    Explore →

  • Gender Equity

    Giving women and girls the tools to reach their potential, and dismantling barriers to girls’ education.

    Explore →

  • Civic Engagement

    Equipping young people with the knowledge and skills to become active citizens in their communities.

Flagship programme

SMILS — our SMART Libraries

SMILS, the SMART Libraries initiative, is MAYEIN’s flagship programme: community libraries and a mobile library van running ICT training, CBT preparation, reading activities and mentoring for young people across Nigeria.
