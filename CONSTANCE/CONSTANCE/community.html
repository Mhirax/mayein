Community Health Focus

Targeted health information for Nigerian and African communities, focusing on prevention and accessible care.

Malaria Prevention

Sleep under insecticide-treated nets (ITNs), clear stagnant water around your home, use approved insect repellents, and seek immediate testing if a fever develops.

Sanitation & Hygiene

Use proper toilet facilities, dispose of waste safely, and keep your immediate surroundings clean to prevent the spread of cholera, typhoid, and other waterborne diseases.

Clean Water Access

Always boil, filter, or chemically treat water before drinking. Store drinking water in clean, covered containers to prevent contamination.

Childhood Vaccination

Ensure children complete their full immunization schedule at the local primary health centre, including BCG, polio, measles, and DPT vaccines.

Maternal & Child Health

Attend all scheduled antenatal care (ANC) visits, deliver with the assistance of a skilled healthcare provider, and breastfeed exclusively for the first 6 months.

Primary Healthcare

Your nearest Primary Health Centre (PHC) offers free or highly subsidized basic services, including immunizations, family planning, and routine health screenings.
