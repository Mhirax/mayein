Prevention & Wellness

Most diseases can be prevented or effectively managed through consistent, everyday healthy habits.

Balanced Nutrition

Eat a variety of colorful vegetables, fruits, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats. Limit added sugars, excessive salt, and highly processed foods to maintain optimal body function.

Regular Exercise

Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate physical activity most days of the week. Walking, jogging, cycling, or dancing all count and significantly improve cardiovascular health.

Quality Sleep

Adults need 7–9 hours of quality sleep per night. Maintain a consistent sleep schedule, keep your bedroom dark and cool, and limit screen time before bed.

Good Hygiene

Wash hands frequently with soap and clean water, especially before eating and after using the restroom. Brush teeth twice daily and keep your living environment clean.

Vaccination

Stay up to date with recommended vaccines for yourself and your children. Vaccines are the safest and most effective way to prevent serious infectious diseases.

Regular Check-ups

Visit a healthcare professional annually for routine screenings, including blood pressure, blood sugar, and cholesterol checks, even if you feel perfectly healthy.
