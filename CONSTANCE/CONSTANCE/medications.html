Medications Information

Learn about prescription medicines, over-the-counter drugs, supplements, and medication safety.

Prescription Medicines

Medications that require a doctor's prescription. Always follow dosage instructions, complete the full course of treatment, and never share prescription drugs with others.

Over-the-Counter (OTC) Medicines

Medicines you can buy without a prescription for common conditions like pain, colds, and allergies. Read labels carefully and follow dosage instructions.

Supplements & Vitamins

Dietary supplements, vitamins, and minerals. While generally safe, consult your healthcare provider before starting any supplement, especially if you take other medications.

Medication Safety

Store medicines properly, check expiration dates, keep medications away from children, and always inform your doctor about all medicines you are taking to avoid dangerous interactions.

Reading Drug Labels

Understanding active ingredients, dosage instructions, warnings, side effects, and storage requirements. Always read the label before taking any medication.

Drug Interactions

Some medicines can interact dangerously with other drugs, foods, or alcohol. Always inform your healthcare provider about all medications, supplements, and herbal products you use.
